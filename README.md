## About Me

I'm a Software Engineering student with a growing interest in **backend development** — especially how APIs, databases, and server-side logic work together.

Right now, I'm learning by building small projects, experimenting with **JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, and MySQL**, and figuring things out one bug at a time. I also enjoy creating Discord bots and exploring how backend systems connect with the frontend.

I'm not an expert yet. Just someone who likes understanding what happens behind the scenes.
